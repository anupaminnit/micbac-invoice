## Invoice Number Series

**Format:** `MIPLWB / [sequence] / [FY]`

| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Prefix** | `MIPLWB` (Micbac India Private Limited, West Bengal) |
| **Sequence** | 3-digit zero-padded number, e.g. `001`, `002`, `003` |
| **Financial Year** | Indian FY format — `YY-YY` (e.g. `25-26`), April start |
| **Separator** | `/` |

**Example:** `MIPLWB/001/25-26`

**Auto-increment logic:** The system reads the last saved invoice number and increments the sequence by 1. The FY suffix resets automatically when the new Indian financial year begins in April.
